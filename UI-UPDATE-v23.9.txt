EDManager v23.9 — Cash calculation alignment

- Passager refunds are deducted from net Passagers, reducing Total théorique and the final Total.
- Décharge and Pré-vendeur are displayed separately while both remain included in the final Total.
- Des acomptes and MEX remain separate components; MEX is counted once in the final Total.
- Excédents remain independent and excluded from all cash totals.
- The live physical cash balance now follows the same signed-operation and employee-advance deduction logic as cash_close_day().
- The cash closure calculation itself is unchanged; it continues to represent expected physical cash, not the commercial Total.
- Existing records, employee permissions, legacy Acompte records, and independent Excédents are preserved.
