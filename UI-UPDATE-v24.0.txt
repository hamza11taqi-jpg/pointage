EDManager v24.0 — Cash accounting consistency

- Centralized the commercial cash totals in one calculation helper so the dashboard, journal printout and closure PDF use the same definition.
- Versement camion displayed = truck cash + MEX.
- Total théorique = truck cash + net Passagers (receipts minus passenger refunds) - Décharge - Pré-vendeur - Des acomptes. It represents the cash that should physically remain in the caisse; MEX is excluded.
- Total Caisse = Total théorique + Décharge + Pré-vendeur + Des acomptes + MEX. Outgoing amounts are added back as supporting proofs, not as new receipts.
- MEX is included once in Versement camion and once in Total Caisse, but never in Total théorique.
- Renamed the dashboard total to “Total Caisse”.
- The closure PDF now distinguishes Total Caisse from Total théorique / expected physical cash, actual counted cash and the difference.
- The journal printout now includes the same cash summary.
- Physical closure reconciliation remains unchanged and matches Total théorique: signed cash operations less employee advances; MEX is excluded because it is not physical cash.
- Existing records, Supabase permissions, legacy Acompte, and independent Excédents are unchanged.

Supabase: no new SQL migration is required for v24.0. Keep the already-applied v23.9 migration.
