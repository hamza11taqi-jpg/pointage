EDManager v24.1 — Cash accounting formula correction
Base: v24.0

- Total théorique is calculated explicitly as truck cash + net Passagers - Décharge - Pré-vendeur - Des acomptes.
- Total Caisse is calculated explicitly as truck cash + net Passagers + MEX.
- MEX is included in displayed Versement camion (cash + MEX), but excluded from physical Total théorique.
- Cash outflows are not added back into Total Caisse.
- Physical closing reconciliation continues to use the signed Supabase cash balance and cash_close_day() calculation.
- No database schema or permission changes.
