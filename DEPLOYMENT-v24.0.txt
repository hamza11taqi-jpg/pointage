4. The service-worker cache name is updated to edmanager-v24-0-cash-accounting so clients fetch the new files.

Accounting definitions:
- Versement camion displayed = truck cash + MEX.
- Total théorique = truck cash + net Passagers - Décharge - Pré-vendeur - Des acomptes. This is the expected physical cash remaining and excludes MEX.
- Total Caisse = Total théorique + Décharge + Pré-vendeur + Des acomptes + MEX.
- Therefore Total Caisse equals gross truck cash + net Passagers + MEX, while the paid-out amounts remain visible as proofs.
- MEX is never included in Total théorique and is counted once in Total Caisse.
